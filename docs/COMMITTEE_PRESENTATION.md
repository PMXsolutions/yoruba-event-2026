## What the public website does

1. **Introduces the celebration** — Yoruba Day Canberra 2026, November 2026, Canberra ACT.  
2. **Credits the Association** — Presented by Yoruba Association Canberra.  
3. **Shares planned cultural highlights** — music, Eyo, cuisine, fashion, storytelling, family — clearly marked as planned / coming soon where needed.  
4. **Invites sponsorship interest** — tier names for conversation; packages and amounts still coming soon.  
5. **Captures Register Interest** — name, contact, party size, preferred ticket type (indicative only).  
6. **Keeps unfinished details honest** — ticketing, programme, date, venue: coming soon or to be confirmed.

---

## How Register Interest works (plain language)

1. A visitor fills in the form on the website.  
2. Their interest is stored securely for the planning committee.  
3. There is **no payment** and **no ticket issued** at this stage.  
4. The committee can later follow up with updates, ticket invitations, and event news.  
5. Confirmation emails can be added once email sending is switched on (Damola).

> Talking point: “Register Interest is our waiting list and planning tool — not a ticket checkout.”

---

## How interest appears in the dashboard

1. Open the **Committee Portal** (`/dashboard`).  
2. Go to **RSVPs** (`/dashboard/rsvps`).  
3. When the database is connected, each Register Interest submission appears as a live record.  
4. Committee members will be able to update status (for example New → Contacted → Confirmed), add notes, tag guests, and export a list.  
5. Until the database connection is finished, the screen may show **presentation sample data** — that is expected and clearly labelled.

## What is live now vs still in development

### Live / ready for review tonight

- Public marketing website  
- Clear messaging: Yoruba Day Canberra 2026 · November 2026 · Canberra ACT  
- Register Interest form (goes live for real submissions after Damola’s database steps)

## How this supports future Yoruba Day events
