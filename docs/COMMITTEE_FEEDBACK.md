# Committee Feedback Framework — Yoruba Day Canberra 2026

Use this guide during or after the committee walkthrough.  
Answers can be verbal, email, or notes against each question.

**Event:** Yoruba Day Canberra 2026  
**Presented by:** Yoruba Association Canberra  
**When:** November 2026 (exact date to be confirmed)  
**Where:** Canberra, ACT (venue to be confirmed)

---

## How to use

1. Assign a note-taker for the meeting.  
2. Work through each section — skip anything not relevant tonight.  
3. Mark decisions as **Agreed**, **Needs research**, or **Defer**.  
4. Feed confirmed content into [CONTENT_CHECKLIST.md](./CONTENT_CHECKLIST.md).

---

## A. Public website

### Wording and tone

- Is the overall wording appropriate for our community, elders, and guests?
- Does anything feel too commercial, too casual, or unclear?
- Should we adjust how we describe “Register Interest” vs ticketing?

### Culture and representation

- Is any important cultural content missing from the Experience section?
- Are references to Eyo, Aso Oke, talking drum, cuisine, and intergenerational storytelling presented respectfully?
- Should we add or remove any planned highlight before public promotion?

### Programme messaging

- Are programme items represented correctly as **planned highlights** (not a final run-of-show)?
- What must be true before we publish a confirmed programme?

### Sponsors

- Are sponsor tier names suitable (Platinum Patron, Gold Circle, Heritage Partner, Community Ally)?
- Is “packages and amounts coming soon” clear enough for potential partners?
- What benefits should we promise only after packages are approved?

### Contact details

- Is `info@yorubadaycanberra.org` the correct public email?
- What phone number should appear when confirmed?
- Which official social channels should we publish first (Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube)?

---

## B. Event planning

### On the day

- What should happen on the day from arrival to closing?
- How long should the celebration run?
- What spaces do we need (main hall, children’s area, vendor area, prayer/quiet space)?

### Cultural performances

- Which cultural performances should be included?
- Who should we approach first (groups, schools, artists)?
- Do we need an MC? Any preferred names?

### Children, youth, and elders

- What should be included for children?
- What should engage youth?
- How do we honour elders in seating, recognition, and programme flow?

### Community partners

- What vendors, performers, or community groups should be approached?
- Are there Association partners or sister organisations to invite early?

### Sponsorship benefits

- What sponsor benefits should we offer (logo placement, speaking slot, tables, programme mention, social posts)?
- Which benefits are non-negotiable for each tier?

---

## C. Platform and committee tools

### Dashboard priorities

- Which committee tasks should the dashboard track first?
- What information should the committee see first when opening the portal?
- Who should have access once sign-in is added (roles)?

### Reports

- What reports would be useful weekly (new interest, follow-ups due, sponsor pipeline)?
- Do we need a simple guest list export for meetings?

### Communications

- What communications should be automated (interest confirmation, ticket invite, reminder, thank you)?
- Which messages must always be approved by a person before sending?
- Preferred channels: email, SMS, WhatsApp, social — in what order?

### Guest follow-up

- Who owns contacting new Register Interest submissions?
- What is our target response time (e.g. within 7 days)?
